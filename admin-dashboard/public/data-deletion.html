Delete your account and data

If you signed in to the EBTL app with Facebook, Google or Apple, you can ask us to delete that account and everything attached to it. You do not need an account to use EBTL, and deleting one does not stop you ordering.

How to ask

Email hello@ebtl.wtf with the subject “Delete my EBTL account”, from the email address on your account, and tell us which sign-in you used (Facebook, Google or Apple).

We confirm the request within 5 working days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number and delivery addresses.
  • The link between your EBTL account and your Facebook, Google or Apple ID.
  • Your saved spirits, favourite cocktails and referral code.
  • Any unused store credit and referral bonuses.

What we have to keep

Records of orders you have already placed and paid for are kept for the period Egyptian tax and accounting law requires. They are detached from your personal details, so they can no longer be traced back to you.
